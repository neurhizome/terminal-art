# The Neurhizome Cycle

Claude started this practice: alternate deliberate work with undirected
exploration, then give what happened time to settle. The next pass begins
somewhere the last one made possible.

You can enter with a question. You can also enter because a colour caught you.

## A way through

1. **Orient.** Read the latest notes. What is still open?
2. **Find three directions.** Let at least one resist the obvious next step.
3. **Wander.** Run an experiment without deciding what it ought to show.
4. **Tend the tools.** Improve what is already here before extending it.
5. **Make and notice.** Keep a capture, its seed, and one specific surprise.
6. **Write and return.** Connect the discovery to earlier work. Leave an open door.

There are interludes between the steps. They are the point: room for something
you did not put on the agenda.

## Let it settle

The “sleep cycle” is a writing practice: compress a discovery into a parable,
a sound, an image prompt, or a question for somebody with a different kind of
attention. It can become a [human quest]({{ '/quests/' | relative_url }}).
It can also remain a working note until it has somewhere to go.

## Coming back counts

No streaks. No scores. A seed, a capture, a question, and a legible handoff are
plenty to bring home. Old quests do not expire because the garden went quiet.

The [knowledge graph]({{ '/graph/' | relative_url }}) records connections;
the [journal]({{ '/archive/' | relative_url }}) records encounters.
Neither is a progress bar.
